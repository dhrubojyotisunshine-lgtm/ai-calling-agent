Draft screens for client approval, based on the live Voxura app (agents.kriscenttechnohub.com), which was reviewed while logged in on 31 Oct 2026. Each screen shows what the app does today and what we propose to build. All names, numbers and figures in the mock screens are sample data. Nothing gets built until you approve.

New in v3 (2 Nov 2026): screens 01–15 are unchanged from v2. Screens 16–32 add the requested sales-automation expansion: from the first enquiry to a paying, onboarded client. These are proposals only; none of them is built yet.
