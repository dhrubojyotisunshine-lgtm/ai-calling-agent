Company level: every row carries workspaceId

Workspace (status, plan, overrides)User + RoleInviteAgentService / ProductLeadCampaignCallTranscriptRecordingFollowUpWalletTransactionCallCost linesActivityLog
  • Already in place: every company record has workspaceId; every query in the built app filters by it, and linked ids are checked to belong to the same company. Automated tests check that company B gets "not found" for company A's records.
  • Added for Super Admin: admin users are separate from company users (an admin is not a member of any company); admin screens see totals, and see a company's leads/calls/recordings only through an approved, time-limited support grant.
  • Recordings are stored per company folder with short-lived signed links, never public URLs.
  • Uniqueness (agent names, lead phones, campaign names) is per company; phone numbers are platform-wide.
C2

Billing architecture (cost, charge and margin per call)

Architecture
1. Call ends. The voice engine reports measured usage: brain tokens in/out, speech-to-text audio seconds, text-to-speech characters, telephony seconds.
2. Cost lines. One CallCost row per provider: usage × ProviderRate valid at call time → Voxura's real cost (AI, STT, TTS, telephony).
3. Customer charge. Billable time × the client's rate (plan or override, B5/B6), using the plan's billing step; unanswered calls free if the plan says so.
4. Wallet. One WalletTransaction per call (already built: atomic, once per call, can't double-charge).
5. Margin = customer charge − sum of cost lines, stored on the call, summed for B1 and B13.
AI brain cost
Speech-to-text cost
Text-to-speech cost
Telephony cost
Customer charge
Profit margin
  • Plan fees and number rent are separate wallet entries with their own cost (number cost from B9).
  • Rates are dated, so re-pricing never changes past calls; money entries are never edited, only reversed.
  • No dummy data: every dashboard figure is a sum over these tables. Until real calls and payments exist the screens show zero and say so.

Build order after approval

  1. Team & Settings (A1–A5): team invites and roles, company profile, password reset, sign-in protection, support-access approval.
  2. Super Admin foundation (B1–B13, C1–C2): admin users and roles, audit log, clients and status, plans and custom pricing, provider rates and access, wallet adjustments, number rent, support access, dashboard and cost/margin reports from real ledgers.
  3. Voice engine and Live call, which starts producing the real usage and cost data these screens report.

Questions for you

  1. Self sign-up for new companies, Voxura-added only, or both?
  2. Plans, prices, billing step (per second / per minute) and whether unanswered calls are free.
  3. Wallet adjustment amount that needs a second approver; lowest margin sales may offer.
  4. Support access: view-only only, or also edit? Emergency access allowed?
  5. Which Voxura people get Super admin, Finance, Support and Read-only roles?
  6. How long to keep audit logs, call recordings and transcripts (data-privacy rules)?

The figures in these mock screens are illustrative only. In the built app, dashboards and reports read only from the database; no hard-coded numbers.
