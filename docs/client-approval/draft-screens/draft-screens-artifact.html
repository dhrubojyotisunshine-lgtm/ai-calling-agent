Screen 4 · Customer

Your services and products

The customer lists everything they sell. Each item is either a service (done for the customer, such as app development) or a product (software the customer buys or subscribes to). Every item has a title and a description. The AI agent uses this list to explain the offer on a call, answer questions, suggest the right option and arrange a follow-up.

  • Two buttons: Add service and Add product
  • Add, edit, switch off or reorder items
  • Optional: price or price range, duration, FAQs and a short "when to offer this" note
  • Choose which services and products each agent may talk about
  • The agent only describes what is written here
Decide
  • Which details per item: starting price, pricing model (fixed, monthly, hourly), timeline, technologies, case studies, brochure?
  • May the agent quote prices, or only say "our team will share a quote"?
  • What happens when a customer shows interest: book a demo, send a proposal by email or WhatsApp, or alert your sales team?
  • Which services or products need a human to explain them?
  • Products only: pricing per user or per month, free trial, and where a customer can buy or sign up?

How the agent uses it on a call

CustomerWe want an app for our delivery staff. What does it cost?
AgentOur mobile apps start from ₹4 lakh and take 8 to 12 weeks. Would you like a demo call with our team this Saturday?
CustomerYes, 11 AM works.
AgentBooked. I will send you a reminder on Friday and our team will share a short proposal after the demo.

If the customer asks about a product, such as the Billing App, the agent explains it, quotes the listed price and offers a free trial link. The demo appears as a task on the lead in the CRM, and a proposal follow-up is scheduled.
