Part 1

The big picture

Voxura is an AI calling assistant for businesses. It gathers a company's leads in one place, lets an AI agent call them in their own language, records what was said, and tells the team what to do next. Every company works in its own private workspace; Voxura's own staff run the platform from the Super Admin.

The end-to-end journey of one lead

  1. The business signs up and sets up

    Creates its company workspace, adds services, sets up an AI agent and gets a phone number from Voxura. (M1, M3, M4, M5)

    Built

  2. A lead arrives

    Typed in by staff, uploaded from Excel, or (after the Lead Source Hub) received automatically from a website, Facebook, Google Ads or IndiaMART. Phone numbers are cleaned and duplicates are blocked. (M7, M8, D2)

    Built (manual & Excel)

  3. The lead is chosen for a campaign

    The owner picks which leads to call, when, and with which agent. DND numbers and leads busy in other campaigns are skipped; cost and wallet are checked first. (M9)

    Built

  4. The AI agent calls

    Within calling hours, the voice engine dials, speaks in the lead's language, answers questions from the business's facts and FAQs, and decides the outcome. (Part 4)

    Needs accounts

  5. The call is recorded and charged

    Recording, transcript, summary and outcome are saved in Call history. Talk time is charged once to the wallet; unanswered calls are never charged. (M10, M12)

    Built (screens & ledger)

  6. The next step is created

    If the lead asked to be called back, a follow-up is created for the AI or a team member. Overdue and today's work appears on the dashboard. (M11, M2)

    Built

  7. The team moves the lead forward

    Staff update the stage (Contacted, Interested, Demo / proposal, Won or Lost), add notes and deal value, and plan the next call. (M7)

    Built

  8. Voxura oversees the platform

    Super Admin sees every company's usage, money and health, assigns numbers, approves wallet credits and keeps an audit trail. (M6, D6)

    Number pool built
Part 2

Users, roles and permissions

Every person belongs to one company workspace and only ever sees that company's data. Within a company there are three roles. Voxura's own staff have a separate Super Admin role.

Owner

The person who signed the company up. Full control of agents, campaigns, leads, numbers and money.

Admin

Trusted manager. Same daily powers as the owner: can spend wallet money by starting campaigns and AI callbacks.

Staff

Sales or support team member. Works leads and team tasks; can view, but not change, anything that costs money.

A person from one company who types another company's record address gets "not found". All of this is covered by the automated checks.

Part 4

How one AI call works

This is the heart of Voxura. The screens, rules and records around the call are built (Parts 3 and 2). The live voice connection is the next technical step and starts as soon as the provider accounts are ready. Needs provider accounts

1Before dialling

  • Inside the campaign's calling hours (IST)?
  • Lead not DND, not busy in another campaign?
  • Wallet above ₹0?
  • Below the "calls at once" limit?
  • Agent active and has a number?
  • Loads the agent's script, FAQs, catalogue, language, voice and what it remembers about this person.

2During the call

  • Dials through the Indian SIP line on the company's number.
  • Listens: speech-to-text (Deepgram).
  • Thinks: AI brain (Claude) answers from the business's facts, offers only approved services, quotes price only if allowed.
  • Speaks: Indian voice (Sarvam) in the chosen language.
  • Handles silence and errors with the agent's own lines; hangs up politely.

3After the call

  • Saves result, outcome, recording, transcript and summary (M10).
  • Charges talk time once to the wallet (M12); no charge if unanswered.
  • Updates campaign progress; schedules a retry if no answer (M9).
  • Creates a callback if asked (M11).
  • Remembers key facts about the caller for next time.

Outgoing calls (campaigns and callbacks)

The campaign or follow-up decides who and when; the agent decides how to speak; the checks above decide whether the call may happen at all.

Incoming calls

A customer dials the company's number; the one agent set to answer that number (M5) picks up, recognises a known lead by number, and the call is recorded and charged the same way.

D2

Lead Source Integration Hub

Integrations → marketplace → connect → logs

Designed
Sources: website form, webhook, custom API, Excel/CSV, Google Sheets, Zapier, Make, WordPress, Google Ads, IndiaMART, Facebook & Instagram lead ads (Meta approval)
  1. Lead submitted at source
  2. Received & verified
  3. Stored once (no doubles on resend)
  4. Cleaned; match by phone, then email
  5. New lead or merge
  6. Assign by rules
  7. Team task + AI call queue

Step by step

  • Connect: open the marketplace, pick a source, follow its guided connect (sign in with Facebook/Google, or copy a webhook URL / API key, shown once).
  • Receive: each delivery is checked for a valid signature and stored once, even if the source sends it again.
  • Match: phone first, then email, within the company only.
  • Merge: fills only empty fields, never overwrites; keeps every source and a change history.
  • Assign: rules by source, campaign, location, language or category pick the AI agent and the salesperson.
  • Consent: records where and when the lead agreed, and on which channels; DND is checked.
  • Hand-off: creates a team task and puts the lead in the AI queue. Calls are not started automatically unless the company switches that on.

Reliability & monitoring

  • Failed deliveries retry automatically with growing gaps.
  • Integration logs show every lead received, with a step-by-step trace, and "fix & retry".
  • Health status per integration; failure alert email.
  • Keys and passwords stored encrypted; API keys stored only as hashes.

Database groundwork already done: the tables for integrations, received events, sources, history, assignment rules and background jobs are in place.

Final outcome: leads from every channel arrive in the CRM within seconds, cleaned, merged, assigned and ready for the AI and the team, with full traceability.

Part 7

Automation behind the scenes

Two kinds of automation. Instant automation runs at the moment someone acts and is built today. Scheduled automation runs on its own in the background and comes with the job runner (Phase 0) and the voice engine.

Instant Built

  • Phone number cleaning into one standard format (leads, import, search, numbers).
  • Duplicate blocking per company (leads, agents, services, campaigns, numbers).
  • Excel column detection, preview counts, never-overwrite import.
  • Live audience count, cost estimate and wallet check while building a campaign.
  • DND and busy-lead exclusion; DND blocks AI callbacks.
  • Status guards on campaigns and follow-ups, rechecked at the moment of the click.
  • Wallet: balance + ledger in one step; one charge per call.
  • Follow-up buckets (Overdue/Today/Upcoming) in India time.
  • Unanswered 0-second calls relabelled "No answer".
  • Moving an agent between numbers; unlinking agents when a number is taken back.
  • Safe CSV export; safe recording links; safe return links.

Scheduled Planned / voice engine

  • Campaign dialler: starts at the start time, respects hours and limits, retries, completes.
  • AI callbacks at their due time.
  • Auto-pause campaigns when the wallet reaches ₹0.
  • Monthly number rent and plan charges.
  • Low-balance, rent-due and task-due emails.
  • Integration sync (IndiaMART pull, Sheets) and retry of failed deliveries.
  • Lead scoring refresh; automation rules.
  • Support access expiry; daily summary emails.
  • Failed-job monitor in Super Admin.

Design rule: no screen refreshes itself in a loop. Pages update when the user acts, which keeps the live server light.

Part 8

Business rules, security and data safety

Company isolation

  • Every record belongs to one company; every query is limited to it.
  • Names and numbers are unique per company, not across Voxura.
  • Tested with two companies trying to reach each other's data.

Compliance

  • DND respected by default (TRAI).
  • Calls only within calling hours.
  • Consent evidence per lead (D2).
  • Recording on/off per agent.

Money

  • Prepaid: no calls at ₹0.
  • Charged once per call, to the paisa.
  • Unanswered calls are free.
  • Large admin credits need two people.

Accounts

  • Passwords stored as secure hashes.
  • Signed session, checked on every page.
  • Role checks on the server.
  • Planned: 2-step sign-in, lockout.

India first

  • All times in IST; amounts in ₹ with Indian commas.
  • Indian mobile, landline and toll-free formats.
  • 11 Indian languages.

Records kept

  • Deleting a lead or campaign keeps call records.
  • Ledger lines are never edited.
  • Planned: audit log of every admin action.
